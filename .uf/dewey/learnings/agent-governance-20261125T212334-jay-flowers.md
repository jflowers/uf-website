When replacing a mandatory workflow with a tiered rubric in an agent-instruction document, sweep the entire document and public content for the old model's arithmetic and qualifiers — tier counts ("Both tiers"), exemption lists, "(Mandatory)" headers, and marketing claims about the old guarantee. Each one becomes a live contradiction the moment the flip lands.
