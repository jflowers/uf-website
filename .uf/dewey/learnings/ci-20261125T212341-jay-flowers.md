In repositories whose only CI workflow triggers on push to main (no PR checks), "CI green" as a PR-time acceptance criterion is operationally defined by the CI Parity Gate's local replication, not by check-run results — policy docs stating acceptance criteria should cross-reference that gate to keep the contract checkable.
